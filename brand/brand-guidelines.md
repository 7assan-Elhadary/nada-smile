## Logo

The supplied logo is the source-of-truth visual identity for the website.

Files:
- `logo-primary.png` — primary light-background lockup.
- `logo-dark.png` — dark-background lockup.
- `logo-icon.png` — icon/NS mark presentation.
- `favicon.png` — favicon-ready crop.
- `logo-board-reference.png` — original supplied brand board for visual reference.

The SVG files in this package are **embedded-raster SVG wrappers**, not true vector redraws. Do not claim they are vector artwork. If a true vector logo is later supplied, replace these files without changing the UI.

### Usage
- Prefer the primary logo on light backgrounds.
- Use the dark-background logo only on sufficiently dark sections.
- Keep the logo clear of busy imagery.
- Do not distort, rotate, recolor, or add effects to the logo.
- Do not recreate the logo with a different font.
- Preserve adequate clear space around the mark.

## Typography

Use a modern, highly readable sans-serif.
Recommended:
- Latin: Inter
- Arabic: IBM Plex Sans Arabic or Noto Sans Arabic

Typography should feel medical, precise and calm — not futuristic to the point of looking like a gaming/crypto product.

Suggested hierarchy:
- H1: 48–64px desktop, 36–44px mobile, weight 600–700
- H2: 32–44px desktop, 28–34px mobile
- Body: 16–18px, line-height 1.7–1.9
- Small/meta: 13–14px

## UI language

Use:
- generous whitespace
- medium-radius cards
- subtle borders
- restrained shadows
- calm hover/focus states
- small, purposeful motion

Avoid:
- generic SaaS template appearance
- excessive gradients
- excessive glassmorphism
- neon effects
- huge rounded pills everywhere
- overuse of stock dental imagery
- excessive animation

## Photography direction

If images are added:
- bright, clean clinical environments
- authentic dental care
- natural human expressions
- clean instruments and technology
- avoid frightening procedures
- avoid overly staged stock-photo compositions

## Accessibility
- Maintain WCAG-conscious contrast.
- Never use color as the only status indicator.
- Visible keyboard focus states.
- Semantic HTML.
- Form labels must remain accessible.
- RTL must be correct for Arabic.
